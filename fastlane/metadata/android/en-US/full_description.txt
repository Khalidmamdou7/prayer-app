Azan is a calm, ad-free prayer times app. No ads, no tracking, no account.

ACCURATE PRAYER TIMES
• Times are downloaded from aladhan.com when you're online and stored for the coming weeks, so the app keeps working offline.
• Without internet, times are calculated on your phone.
• 20 calculation methods, including Egyptian, Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Moonsighting Committee, Karachi, Gulf, Turkey, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Jordan, Malaysia, Indonesia, France and Russia.
• Standard or Hanafi Asr.
• Shift any time by a few minutes to match your local mosque.

ADHAN OR NOTIFICATION, PER PRAYER
• For each prayer, choose the full adhan, a notification, or nothing.
• Pick a built-in adhan voice, any recording on your phone, or your alarm sound.
• Stop the adhan from the notification at any time.

"HAVE YOU PRAYED?"
• Before each prayer's time ends (20 minutes by default), Azan gently asks whether you've prayed it.
• Answer from the notification. If you've already ticked the prayer, there's no reminder.

ALWAYS KNOW WHAT'S NEXT
• A live countdown to the next prayer.
• Time remaining for every prayer.
• Today's progress at a glance.
• Gregorian and Hijri dates.

FIND YOUR CITY OFFLINE
• Search 34,000 cities in English or Arabic, or use your location.
• Picking a city sets the usual calculation method for that country.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH
• Full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout and Arabic digits. Switch languages in Settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no analytics and no account.
• Your settings stay on your phone.
• For online sync, only an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent to aladhan.com. You can turn sync off.
